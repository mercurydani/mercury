Questions

How long does it take?

You get the first version of a landing page within a few days, and a Mini store in 7–10 days. For bigger projects we agree on the deadline upfront.

Who pays for the domain and hosting?

Domain and hosting are paid separately, directly to the provider. We help you choose and set them up.

Can I change prices and texts myself?

Yes. We set up an admin panel where you edit products, prices and texts yourself — no developer needed.

I already have a website. Do you start from scratch?

No. We work with WordPress, MODX, Tilda and plain code: we fix, speed up and add what's missing.

Are the prices on the site final?

Prices are starting points. We give an exact quote after a short free call and agree on it upfront, with no hidden costs.

What does support include?

Basic: small edits up to 2 hours a month, backups, updates and uptime monitoring. Plus: up to 6 hours a month and a same-day reply.

When will SEO show results?

First changes in Google usually appear within 1–3 months. We don't promise first place: the report shows which searches the site climbed for and how many enquiries came in.
